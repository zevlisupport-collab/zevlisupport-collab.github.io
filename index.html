Delete your ZEVLI account

You can request deletion of your ZEVLI account and the personal data associated with it at any time.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email from the email address associated with your ZEVLI account.
  2. Use the subject line ZEVLI Account Deletion Request.
  3. Ask us to delete your ZEVLI account and associated personal data.
Request account deletion

Support email: zevlisupport@gmail.com

What data is deleted?

When your account deletion request is completed, ZEVLI deletes or de-identifies personal data associated with your account, including profile information, preferences, photos and videos stored by ZEVLI, messages, reactions, matches, parent/social posts and comments, activity information, and notification or device tokens.

Data that may be retained

Account deletion requests are generally completed within 30 days. Limited records may remain for up to 90 days in backups, security logs, or fraud-prevention systems.

Certain transaction, billing, tax, legal, safety, consent, fraud, abuse-prevention, or dispute-resolution records may be retained longer when required by law or for legitimate security and compliance purposes.

Google Play subscriptions

Deleting your ZEVLI account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. If you have a subscription, cancel it separately through Google Play to prevent future renewal charges.
