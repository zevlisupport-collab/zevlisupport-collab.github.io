ZEVLI Child Safety Standards

Last updated: September 14, 2026

ZEVLI is an adults-only platform intended for users age 18 and older. ZEVLI has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Prohibited Content and Conduct

ZEVLI strictly prohibits any content, communication, or behavior that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers a child. This includes CSAM, grooming, sexualization of minors, sextortion involving minors, child sex trafficking, solicitation of minors for sexual purposes, and attempts to create, upload, distribute, request, or exchange CSAM.

Reporting and Enforcement

ZEVLI provides users with in-app reporting tools for reporting profiles, messages, content, and safety concerns. Reports involving suspected CSAE or CSAM are treated as high-priority safety matters.

ZEVLI may remove prohibited content, restrict or permanently terminate accounts, preserve relevant information when legally required, and cooperate with appropriate law-enforcement and child-safety authorities.

CSAM and Authorities

When ZEVLI obtains actual knowledge of apparent CSAM, ZEVLI will take appropriate action in accordance with applicable law, including removal or restriction of the material and reporting confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the appropriate regional authority when required.

Adults Only

ZEVLI is intended only for people age 18 or older. Accounts determined to belong to minors may be removed or restricted.

Child Safety Contact

Child-safety concerns and official communications regarding CSAE or CSAM may be sent to ZEVLI's designated child-safety contact.
